# Seeds of Reflection

Seeds of Reflection

Supporting meaningful family conversations about gender, stereotypes, and AI through reflection and play.

DOI License: CC BY 4.0

Seeds of Reflection is a cultural probe that supports conversations within families about gender bias in AI-generated imagery. It combines reflection, discussion, play, and creative activities to help parents and children explore stereotypes, representation, and the ways AI systems shape everyday understandings of gender.

The toolkit was developed as part of Tadaa, a research project on how children and parents can critically engage with generative AI and its representations of people.

Who it is for

Families with children. The toolkit can be used:

at home
in workshops
in educational settings
in research activities
What's inside

Ten activities in two phases, plus an instruction leaflet:

Phase	Activities	For
1. Self-reflection	5 activities	Parents and caregivers
2. Shared activities	5 activities	The whole family
Instruction leaflet: guidance for each activity and prompts to support reflection and discussion.
Parent activities: invite caregivers to document experiences, assumptions, and reflections related to gender and technology.
Family activities: discussions, games, and creative exercises using AI-generated images, building on the parents' reflections.

How to use it
Read the instruction leaflet first.
Start with the parent activities. Caregivers work through the five self-reflection activities on their own.
Move on to the family activities. Use the parents' reflections as a starting point for the shared discussions, games, and creative exercises.

The activities can be spread over several sessions or adapted into a single workshop, so participants can go at their own pace. Preparation is minimal.

There are no right or wrong answers: the toolkit is meant to open conversations and encourage exploration, not to test anyone.
